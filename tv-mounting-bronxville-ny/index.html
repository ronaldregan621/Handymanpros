Local Stories

Typical TV Walls in Bronxville, NY

The rooms and wall types we plan for across Lawrence Park, Sagamore Park and the village center. Use these to plan your own TV wall.

Above a stone fireplace in a Lawrence Park Tudor

Lawrence Park Tudors often have stone or brick fireplaces with a tall chimney breast. The right way is to anchor into the mortar joints, not the face of the stone, and to use a pull down or tilt mount so the screen comes down to a comfortable seated height. Wires run into the wall cavity beside the chimney where it is open, or into a painted raceway where it is not.

A prewar co op near the station

Many Bronxville apartments are in prewar buildings where the walls are plaster over block or concrete, not studs. Those walls need masonry anchors and slow drilling to avoid cracking the plaster, and most buildings want a certificate of insurance before any work. We send the certificate to your managing agent ahead of the visit and work within the building's hours.

Samsung Frame in a renovated family room

Renovated kitchens, family rooms and additions in Bronxville are usually drywall, which is the best wall for a Samsung Frame. It mounts flush with no gap, the One Connect box tucks into a nearby cabinet or console, and the single cord runs inside the wall, so the Frame reads as a painting when it is off.

What Bronxville homeowners ask about

Walls change room to room. A Tudor can have plaster in the living room and drywall in a finished basement, and a co op can have concrete behind every wall. We bring hardware for plaster, masonry and drywall on every visit, so the wall type never turns a quick job into a return trip.

Bronxville TV Mounting FAQ

Common questions from homeowners and co op residents across Bronxville, NY.

Do you mount TVs on plaster and masonry walls in Bronxville? +
Yes. Much of Bronxville is prewar construction with plaster walls, masonry fireplaces and, in many apartments, concrete or block behind the plaster. We check the wall first, then use the correct anchors so the mount is secure and the plaster stays intact.
Can you mount a TV above a fireplace in a Bronxville home? +
Yes. Above the fireplace mounts are common in Lawrence Park and Sagamore Park homes with brick or stone hearths. We anchor into the mortar joints, use a tilt or pull down mount for comfortable viewing, and route wires cleanly.
Can you hide wires in an older Bronxville home? +
Often yes. In drywall additions and finished basements we route in wall for a fully clean finish. In plaster or masonry we use a slim paintable raceway matched to the wall color. Either way the result looks intentional, not patched.
