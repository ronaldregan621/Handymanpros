What We Install

Aging in Place Services in Wilton

Most falls in the home happen in the bathroom, on stairs, and at uneven thresholds. We install the modifications that address each of those locations directly, using hardware rated for the load and anchored into studs or proper backing material so the installation holds under real use.

Grab Bar Installation

ADA compliant grab bars beside the toilet, in the shower, and next to the tub. Anchored into wall studs or with backing plates where studs are not accessible. Rated for 250 lbs minimum load.

Handrail Reinforcement

Interior and exterior handrail installation and tightening. We add graspable rails on both sides of staircases where only one exists and secure loose existing rails that have worked free of the wall.

Threshold Ramps

Rubber and aluminum threshold ramps over raised door saddles that create a trip hazard. Common at exterior doors, garage entries, and between different flooring surfaces on the same level.

Toilet Safety Rails

Freestanding and wall mounted toilet safety rails that provide push up support. Important for post surgical recovery and for anyone with hip or knee mobility limitations.

Lever Door Hardware

Round door knobs replaced with lever style handles throughout the home. Levers do not require gripping and twisting and are significantly easier for hands with arthritis or reduced grip strength.

Safety Lighting

Motion activated night lights and improved fixtures in hallways, stairwells, and bathrooms. Most nighttime falls happen on routes between the bedroom and bathroom with inadequate lighting.

FAQ

Aging in Place Questions for Wilton Homeowners

Can you anchor grab bars into tile without cracking it?
Yes. We drill tile with the correct bit and anchor into the studs or solid backing behind the tile surround so the bar holds full load without stressing the tile. This is one of the most common Wilton requests and we do it routinely. We confirm the layout with you before drilling.
Can you combine aging in place work with other handyman tasks?
Yes. We regularly combine grab bar installation with furniture assembly, TV mounting, and smoke detector service in a single visit. If you have a list of tasks, call (475) 500-7126 before booking so we can confirm what fits in one trip.
Do I need a doctor's recommendation to book aging in place modifications?
No. You can book directly without a referral or recommendation. Occupational therapist assessments are useful for complex accessibility projects but are not required for standard grab bar and handrail installation.
How quickly can you come to Wilton?
Same day appointments are available most days in Wilton. If a family member is being discharged from a hospital or rehab facility, we can often schedule around that date to have modifications in place before they return home.
