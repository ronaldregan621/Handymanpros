Childproofing Services in Fairfield

The American Academy of Pediatrics reports that babies begin pulling up to standing around 8 to 10 months and become independently mobile very quickly after. The window to complete safety installations before a child has access to the full home is measured in weeks. We work with Fairfield families to close that window before any incidents happen.

22,500
Children injured annually by furniture tip overs in the United States, according to the Consumer Product Safety Commission. One child dies every two weeks from a furniture or TV tip over. Anti tip anchoring to wall studs is the most direct preventive measure available.

Hardware Mounted Baby Gates

Anchored into wall studs or a solid doorframe post at the top and bottom of every staircase. We never use pressure mounted gates at stair applications. Includes metal and wood frame options.

Furniture Anti Tip Anchoring

Dressers, bookshelves, armoires, and TV consoles anchored to wall studs with CPSC rated anti tip straps. Includes stud location, proper hardware selection, and load testing before we leave.

Cabinet and Drawer Locks

Magnetic and spring loaded locks on lower cabinets containing cleaning products, medications, sharp utensils, and heavy cookware. Kitchen and bathroom priority locations first.

Outlet Covers and Plates

Tamper resistant outlet covers throughout the home. We prioritize outlets at child height in living areas, bedrooms, and hallways. Also includes sliding plate covers for outlets already in use.

Corner and Edge Guards

Foam and rubber edge guards on coffee tables, dining tables, and sharp furniture corners at head height for a standing toddler. Adhesive backed and removable without wall damage.

Window Stop Locks and Cord Management

Window stops that limit opening to 4 inches per fall prevention guidelines. Blind cord wind ups and replacement with cordless hardware to eliminate strangulation hazard from looped cords.

FAQ

Childproofing Questions for Fairfield Homeowners

Should baby gates be hardware mounted or pressure mounted?
Hardware mounted gates are required at the top of any staircase. Pressure mounted gates are appropriate only for room dividers and low risk doorways. We use hardware mounted gates at all stair applications in Fairfield homes.
Do you anchor furniture to plaster walls in Fairfield?
Yes. Many older Southport and Greenfield Hill homes have plaster and lath walls. We use appropriate toggle anchors and backing plates for plaster installations so the anti tip strap holds the same load as a stud anchored install.
Can I combine childproofing with furniture assembly or TV mounting in one visit?
Yes. We regularly combine childproofing with furniture assembly, TV mounting, and other tasks in a single visit. If you have multiple services to combine, call (475) 500-7126 before booking so we can confirm what fits in one trip to your Fairfield home.
What age should I childproof my home?
Before the child has access to the space. For furniture anchoring and outlet covers, before you move in or before a baby begins crawling. For baby gates, before the child can walk or climb stairs independently. The AAP recommends hardware gates at stairs from the first day a child has stair access.
