Before You Book

What Makes an Outdoor TV Install Different

Why it costs more than an indoor mount

An indoor TV on drywall starts at $199. Outside, the job changes. The wall is siding, stucco, brick or stone instead of drywall. Every hole we make has to be sealed so water does not get behind the siding. The hardware has to resist rust. The TV is often going up high under a porch roof or onto a post, and the cables have to reach an outdoor outlet without becoming a trip hazard. It takes longer and the stakes are higher, so the starting price is higher.

Where the TV can go

  • Covered porch or patio: the best spot. The roof keeps direct rain and midday sun off the screen.
  • Exterior house wall: works on vinyl, wood and fiber cement siding when we anchor into the framing behind it, never the siding alone.
  • Brick, stone or concrete: very solid once anchored. This is the +$75 surface.
  • Pool house, pergola or outdoor kitchen: text a photo. A post or beam has to be sized to carry the TV, and we will tell you if yours is.

Outdoor TV or regular TV?

We mount whichever you buy, but you should know the difference. Outdoor-rated TVs such as the Samsung Terrace or SunBrite are sealed against moisture and are much brighter, so you can see the picture in daylight. A regular indoor TV is not built for humidity, pollen or Connecticut winters, and most manufacturers will not cover one that has lived outside. If you use an indoor TV, keep it under a solid roof and plan on a weatherproof cover or enclosure. Pick a location that is shaded in the afternoon either way. Glare is the number one complaint with patio TVs.

Mount, power and wires

The mount is not included in the $540. Bring your own or add ours for $115. A full-motion mount is popular outside because you can turn the screen toward the seating area or the pool. For power, the TV needs an outdoor outlet within reach of its cord. If there is no outlet near the spot you want, tell us when you text, since adding one is not part of this price. Cables can be tidied in an exterior cord cover for $50. Any other materials your install needs are quoted before the job.
