### Why a ~30% Premium Works Here

- **Fairfield / Greenwich psychology**  
  - High-end clients pay for **“no compromises”**: plaster-safe, invisible wires, tested Wi‑Fi cams, clean furniture assembly.  
  - You’re selling peace of mind vs “just a handyman.”  

- **Value ladder design**  
  - **Base prices** cover perfection-level labor and insurance.  
  - **Add-ons** (wires, brick, fireplaces, heavy items) fund complexity without surprise sticker shock.  
  - Competitors hide this; you show it clearly and win trust.  

- **Guarantee-backed premium**  
  - Anchor pricing to a bold promise: **“Lifetime redo free if anything shifts, breaks, or wobbles.”**  
  - No major competitor is pairing this level of guarantee with transparent pricing.  

- **Competitor gap**  
  - **Mr Home Guy / HelloTech**: mid-market volume in the ~$139–$250 band, upsell-heavy, vague on add-ons.  
  - **QuickMountTV / NYC TV Mounting**: strong brand trust but thinner, less-local content and less transparent pricing.  
  - **You**: hyperlocal Fairfield specialist, Reddit-informed pain-point copy, **transparent premium** pricing that matches your positioning.
