The Wall Problem: What Westchester Homes Are Actually Made Of

This is the detail most handymen don't think about until they're already on the job. Westchester has an enormous amount of older housing stock. The county was heavily developed from the 1920s through the 1960s, and many of those homes still have original tile bathrooms. What you find behind that tile is often a surprise.

In newer construction, you drill through tile and hit drywall, then find studs or use proper toggle anchors. In older Westchester homes, you drill through tile and hit plaster, which behaves completely differently. Plaster cracks if you use the wrong bit at the wrong speed. Plaster doesn't hold the same anchors that work in drywall. And plaster over tile means you need to apply completely different technique to avoid cracking the grout lines and compromising the waterproofing of the bathroom surround.

Our installers know the difference between a 1950s Yonkers co-op bathroom and a 2005 New Rochelle renovation. We bring the right drill bits, the right anchors, and the right approach for whatever we find in the wall. If a wall isn't safe for a permanent grab bar, we'll tell you that before we start drilling, not after.

What to Expect from a Westchester Grab Bar Installation

When we show up, the first thing we do is assess the walls. We identify stud locations with a stud finder, confirm the wall construction, and discuss placement with whoever is home. Standard placement follows American Disabilities Association guidelines: grab bars in the shower typically go 33 to 36 inches above the floor, angled bars go on the back wall of the shower surround, and toilet grab bars go on the wall closest to the toilet at the same height range.

If you already have bars selected, we'll install them. If you need recommendations, we carry a small inventory of commonly used bars and can order specific models. The installation itself involves finding studs or installing structural backing, drilling, setting the flange with stainless screws, and testing the bar for load. We test every bar to 250 pounds before we leave.

Our grab bar installation service includes a full walkthrough with the resident before we go. We make sure they know how to use the bar correctly and feel confident doing so. That last step matters as much as the installation itself.
