Is Your TV Mounted Too High? Here's How to Tell

Published January 25, 2026 by Alliance Handyman Pros

Walk into most living rooms and you'll notice the same thing: TVs mounted way too high above the fireplace. It looks nice until you actually sit down to watch something and realize you're craning your neck like you're in the front row of a movie theater.

So why does everyone do it?

Why TVs End Up Mounted Too High

People mimic what they see at bars and sports venues, where screens have to clear crowds. That makes sense when fifty people are standing around watching the game. It makes zero sense in your living room.

Fireplaces force the issue by putting the only logical wall space above the mantel. Open floor plans limit your options even more. Some folks treat the TV like artwork, centering it on the wall at standing eye level. Others underestimate how large modern screens actually are, so a low mount looks awkward at first. There's a whole community on Reddit called r/TVTooHigh dedicated to this exact problem.

Proper TV Mounting Height Guidelines

The fix is simpler than you think. Sit in your usual viewing spot and measure from the floor to your eyes. That measurement is where the center of your screen should be.

For most people on a couch, that's about 42 inches from the floor. If you have a 55 inch TV, aim for 61 inches to the center of the screen. For a 65 inch TV, about 65 inches to center. If you sit farther back or like to recline, adjust slightly higher.

The goal is looking straight ahead at the screen without tilting your head up. Do that for a two hour movie with your TV mounted too high and you'll feel it in your neck the next morning.

The Fix for Fireplace Mounts

If you have your heart set on mounting above the fireplace, a pull down mount is a game changer. When you're ready to watch, you pull the TV down to a comfortable viewing height. When you're done, push it back up and it looks clean against the wall.

We've installed dozens of these across Greenwich, Darien, and Stamford. They work especially well in rooms where the fireplace is the only logical spot for the TV.

Quick Test

Sit in your usual spot on the couch. Look straight ahead at the wall. Your eyes should land on the center of the screen without tilting your head. If you're looking up, it's too high.
