Best TV Mounts for OLED Screens

OLEDs are thin, expensive, and unforgiving. Here’s how to pick a mount that fits the TV and the room.

Updated Jan 13, 2026

OLED TVs look incredible, but they’re also thin, pricey, and not the kind of thing you want wobbling on a bargain mount. The best mount depends on your wall, your viewing angle, and how clean you want the final setup to look.

Quick rule: If you spent OLED money, don’t cheap out on the mount or the install.

Mount types (and when they make sense)

Fixed mount (lowest profile)

Great when you know the height is right and you want the TV tight to the wall. This is the cleanest look.

Tilting mount (best for above-eye-level)

If the TV is going above a fireplace or higher on the wall, tilt helps viewing comfort without adding a lot of depth.

Full-motion mount (flexibility)

Perfect for corners or rooms where you need to pull the TV out and angle it. Just know it adds depth and needs solid anchoring.

What to look for in a good OLED mount

  • Correct VESA compatibility for your TV size/model
  • Weight rating that comfortably exceeds your TV
  • Sturdy arms and locking points (for full-motion mounts)
  • Room for cables without bending or pinching

Don’t forget the “clean wall” plan

Even the best mount looks bad with a cable mess. Plan for cable concealment and device placement so the setup stays tidy.
