How to Choose a TV Mounting Service

If you want a clean install that feels rock solid, this is what to look for before you book.

Updated Jan 13, 2026

TV mounting sounds simple until it isn’t. Wrong hardware, bad placement, sloppy cables, or a mount that isn’t anchored correctly can turn into a headache fast. If you’re hiring someone, here’s how to pick the right team and avoid the stuff you only notice after they leave.

Quick rule: You’re not just paying for “put TV on wall.” You’re paying for clean placement, correct anchoring, tidy cables, and someone who will fix it if anything feels off.

1) Ask how they anchor the mount

A good installer will talk about studs, wall type, and using the correct hardware. If the answer is vague, that’s a red flag.

  • Drywall: should be into studs for most installs.
  • Brick/stone: needs the right anchors and a clean drilling plan.
  • Metal studs / concrete: requires different hardware and experience.

2) Confirm they’ll help you pick the right height

The best installs look intentional. Height depends on your couch, room layout, and whether it’s a living room TV or a bedroom TV. If they’re just “eyeballing it,” you’re gambling.

3) Ask what “clean cables” means to them

Some people call a dangling HDMI cable “fine.” That’s not the look you want. Ask what options they offer:

  • In-wall routing (when possible)
  • Paintable cord covers (when in-wall isn’t a fit)
  • Clean device placement so the wall stays tidy

4) Make sure they’ll test the setup before they leave

A solid installer doesn’t bolt the TV on and disappear. They verify basic functionality and alignment so you don’t discover a problem during movie night.

5) Look for simple scheduling and upfront pricing

If you’re getting “maybe next week” and a vague estimate, it’s usually not going to be smooth. You want clear pricing, clear timing, and quick communication.
