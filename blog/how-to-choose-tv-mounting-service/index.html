How to Choose a TV Mounting Service

What to look for before you hire, so your TV is secure and the wall looks clean when you are done.

Updated Jan 13, 2026

You just bought a new TV. Maybe it is a 75 inch Samsung, an LG OLED, or a Sony Bravia you have been eyeing for months. Now it is sitting in the box in your living room, and you are thinking: do I really want to drill holes in my wall and hope for the best?

Smart move to look for a professional. But not all TV mounting services are created equal. Some people show up late, drill crooked holes, leave cables dangling, and disappear before you notice the problems.

If you are searching for how to choose a tv mounting service near me, it really comes down to safety, experience, and how easy they make the whole process from quote to finished install. The goal is to protect your TV, your walls, and your time while getting a clean, secure, and great looking setup.

Check insurance and guarantees

This one is non negotiable. If someone drops your OLED or drills into something they should not, you want to know they are covered.

Look for a company that carries liability insurance. This protects you if something is damaged during installation. Ask for proof before they start working. Any professional will be happy to show you.

Make sure they guarantee their work in writing. Reputable TV mounters back installations with a warranty or satisfaction guarantee so you are not paying twice if something needs adjustment. If they hesitate or get vague about any of this, that is a red flag.

Look for real experience with different wall types

There is a big difference between a general handyman who also does TV mounting and someone who does it every single day.

Prioritize specialists who are comfortable with different wall materials like drywall, plaster, brick, stone, and metal studs. Each requires specific anchors and techniques. A specialist will know things a generalist will not, like why Samsung Frame TVs need a specific no gap mount or which full motion mounts actually hold up with 85 inch screens.

For large screens or tricky locations like over fireplaces or on accent walls, choose a team that mentions handling oversized TVs and complex mounts. Those jobs often need a two person crew and heavier duty hardware.

Compare reviews and look for photo proof

Five star reviews are great, but pay attention to what people actually say. Read recent reviews on Google and other local platforms for patterns: on time arrival, careful work, clean cable management, and how they handle issues if something goes wrong.

Look for before and after photos or a gallery of past installs. This shows you how clean their mounting and wiring work really is in living rooms, bedrooms, and apartments like yours. If every picture shows visible cables, they are not doing the job right.

A few negative reviews are not necessarily a dealbreaker. How the company responds to them tells you more than the review itself.

Clarify pricing, scope, and what is included

Cheap quotes often hide expensive surprises. A good TV mounting service provides clear, upfront pricing that explains what is included. That means labor, the wall mount itself, in wall wiring, cord covers, and hardware. You should not be surprised by add on fees once they show up.

Before you book, make sure you understand exactly what you are paying for. Is the mount included, or do you need to buy it separately? Does the price include cable concealment, or is that extra? What about outlet installation behind the TV? Are there additional charges for difficult walls like brick or stone?

Ask for a written or itemized quote that covers TV size, mount type (fixed, tilt, or full motion), wire hiding options, and any extras like soundbar mounting or shelf installation. A good TV mounting service gives you an upfront price and sticks to it. No surprises once they see your setup.

Make sure they handle cable management

A mounted TV with cables hanging down the wall looks worse than a TV on a stand. Seriously.

Professional cable concealment options include in wall cable routing (the cleanest option), installing a recessed outlet behind the TV, and paintable cord covers when in wall is not possible.

Ask to see photos of their previous work. Clean lines, no visible wires, no mess. That is what you are paying for.

Evaluate convenience and communication

You should not have to wait two weeks to get your TV on the wall. Favor companies that offer easy online booking, text or email confirmations, and narrow arrival windows or same day and next day service. That signals an organized operation that respects your schedule.

Pay attention to how quickly and clearly they respond to questions about your wall type, building rules, or parking. Strong communication usually translates into smoother, faster installs.

Also, do they actually answer their phone? If you cannot reach them before the job, good luck reaching them after if something goes wrong.

Do they clean up after themselves

Mounting a TV creates dust and debris, especially when drilling into brick or stone. A professional TV mounting service should use drop cloths to protect your floors and furniture, vacuum up any dust, and remove all packaging and debris when they leave.

You should not have to clean your house after paying someone to do a job.

The bottom line

Choosing a TV mounting service comes down to finding someone who takes the work seriously. You want a specialist, not someone who treats it as an afterthought. You want clear pricing, proper insurance, and someone who shows up when they say they will.

Your TV is an investment. The installation should be too.
