What We Install

Every Hazard. One Appointment.

A professional childproofing visit covers every room based on your child's current and upcoming mobility stage. We bring the hardware, the right anchors for your wall type, and the knowledge of what actually fails vs. what just looks safe.

Critical
🚪

Baby Gate Installation

Hardware-mounted gates at all stairway openings, anchored into studs. Pressure-mounted gates at room divider locations. We do not use pressure-mounted gates at stair tops, a known fall hazard. Includes testing with full adult body weight before we leave.

Critical
🪑

Furniture Anchoring

Anti-tip straps on every tall dresser, bookcase, wardrobe, and freestanding shelving unit. TV wall anchors where TVs are on stands. Confirmed stud-to-stud anchoring, not drywall anchors. IKEA MALM, HEMNES, KALLAX, PAX, and BESTA units all require this and almost none leave the store with the straps installed.

🔌

Outlet Covers

Tamper-resistant outlet plate replacement throughout the home at crawling and early-walking height. Sliding plate covers on regularly used outlets. Full outlet replacement with tamper-resistant receptacles available where code and safety require it.

🗄️

Cabinet and Drawer Locks

Magnetic locks on lower kitchen cabinets containing cleaning products, sharp items, and heavy cookware. Drawer stops on knife drawers and utility drawers. Bathroom cabinet locks for medications, cleaning supplies, and razors. Invisible magnetic systems available for clients who do not want visible hardware on cabinetry.

📐

Corner and Edge Guards

Foam or clear silicone corner guards on coffee tables, console tables, hearth edges, and any sharp furniture corner at head height for a standing toddler. Clear guards available for premium furniture where visible foam is not acceptable aesthetically.

🪟

Window and Cord Safety

Window stop locks that limit opening to 4 inches on all upper-floor windows. Cord wind-ups and cord cleats to remove looped blind cords, the leading cause of strangulation in children under 6. Door pinch guards on interior doors. Door handle covers on rooms that should stay closed.

Common Questions

What does a professional childproofing visit include? +
A professional childproofing visit covers baby gate installation at stairways and room entrances, furniture anchoring with anti-tip straps, cabinet and drawer locks, outlet covers, corner and edge guards, cord and blind cord management, door knob covers, and window stop locks. We walk the home room by room and address every hazard based on your child's current mobility stage.
What is the difference between pressure-mounted and hardware-mounted baby gates? +
Pressure-mounted gates use tension against walls and are appropriate for room dividers and low-risk doorways. Hardware-mounted gates are anchored directly into wall studs or a solid doorframe and are required at the top of any staircase. Using a pressure-mounted gate at the top of stairs is a documented fall hazard. We install hardware-mounted gates at every stair application and confirm stud location before mounting.
Do I need to anchor my furniture? +
Yes. The CPSC reports that furniture tip-overs injure approximately 22,500 children per year. One child dies every two weeks from a furniture or TV tip-over. Dressers, bookshelves, and TVs on stands are the most common culprits. Anti-tip anchoring straps installed into wall studs eliminate this hazard entirely and take about 15 minutes per piece.
How far in advance should I schedule before the baby arrives? +
We recommend 4 to 6 weeks before the due date. Childproofing is most comprehensive when done before the baby is mobile, which happens faster than most parents expect. We offer same-day availability for urgent situations, but advance scheduling ensures a thorough walk-through with no pressure.
Can you childproof a rental apartment? +
Yes. Most childproofing is fully reversible. Cabinet locks, outlet covers, corner guards, and pressure-mounted gates leave no permanent marks. Hardware-mounted gate installations leave small anchor holes that are easily patched. Furniture anchoring uses wall stud anchors that can be removed and the small hole spackled. We have completed full childproofing packages in apartments throughout Fairfield County with no lease complications.
