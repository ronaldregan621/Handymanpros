TV Mounting

TV Mounting Done Right

This is what we do best. Samsung, LG, and Sony TVs mounted on drywall, plaster, brick, stone, or above your fireplace. We show up with the right bracket, the right tools, and we don't leave until it's perfect.

🖼️

Samsung Frame Installation

The Frame is supposed to look like art when it's off, but only if it's mounted perfectly flush to the wall. We do the no-gap mount, hide the One Connect box somewhere smart, and run all cables invisibly. When it's displaying art, your guests won't know it's a TV. When it's on, it's a gorgeous 4K display. That's the whole point of spending the extra money on a Frame.

📱

OLED & Large Format TVs

LG OLED, Sony Bravia, and Samsung Neo QLED TVs are expensive and need to be mounted right the first time. We use heavy duty anchors, hit the studs, and make sure it's rock solid. We've mounted 65", 75", 77", and 85" screens. Your $3,000 TV deserves better than a wobbly mount from the big box store.

🔌

Cable Concealment

Nobody wants to see a mess of cables hanging below their beautiful mounted TV. We run power and HDMI through the wall when possible, install outlets behind the TV, and use paintable cord covers when in-wall isn't doable. Clean lines, no visible wires, no mess.
