TV Mounting

TV Mounting Done Right

Samsung, LG, and Sony TVs mounted on drywall, plaster, brick, stone, or above your fireplace. We show up with the right bracket, the right tools, and we don't leave until it's perfect.

Samsung Frame Installation

The Frame is supposed to look like art when it's off, but only if it's mounted perfectly flush to the wall. We do the no-gap mount, hide the One Connect box somewhere smart, and run all cables invisibly. When it's displaying art, your guests won't know it's a TV.

OLED & Large Format TVs

LG OLED, Sony Bravia, and Samsung Neo QLED TVs need to be mounted right the first time. We use heavy duty anchors, hit the studs, and make sure it's rock solid. We've mounted 65", 75", 77", and 85" screens.

Cable Concealment

We run power and HDMI through the wall when possible, install outlets behind the TV, and use paintable cord covers when in-wall isn't doable. Clean lines, no visible wires, no mess.
