## The one rule that makes citations work: identical NAP everywhere
Google compares your Name, Address, Phone, and Website across every directory. Even "St." vs "Street" or a different phone format weakens the signal. Copy the block below verbatim into every platform. Do not improvise.

```
Business name:  Alliance Handyman Pros
Phone:          (475) 500-7126
Website:        https://www.alliancehandymanpros.com
Service area:   Fairfield County, CT and Westchester, NY
Address:        NONE — service-area business. Choose "I serve customers at their
                location" / "hide my address" on every platform. Do NOT enter a
                street address anywhere (a mismatched address hurts more than none).
Hours:          Every day 8am to 11pm (matches Google Business Profile)
Primary category: Handyman
```

## Do them in this order (highest leverage first)

### 1. Apple Business Connect  →  business.apple.com
The one that puts you on **iPhone Maps and Siri**. iPhone users never touch Google Maps, so today they cannot find you. Highest priority.
- Sign in with your Apple ID. Register the business, pick **Handyman / Home Services**.
- Set it as a service-area business (no storefront shown).
- Verify by phone or postcard. Add the description + hours + website above.

### 2. Bing Places  →  bingplaces.com
Free, and the fastest one: it can **import your whole listing from Google Business Profile in one click**. Do GBP first, then this is 2 minutes.
- Sign in with a Microsoft account → "Import from Google My Business" → confirm the NAP matches the block above.

### 3. Yelp  →  biz.yelp.com
- Search first to see if a listing already exists; **claim it** if so, otherwise create one.
- Categories: Handyman, TV Mounting, Furniture Assembly.
- Free listing only. Ignore the sales calls trying to sell ads.

### 4. Angi  →  angi.com (Business Center)
- This is a **paid lead-gen** platform, not just a citation. A basic profile is a valid citation, but expect aggressive calls trying to sell you leads. Lowest priority of the core four.
