The Short Answer

Three Ways to Hide TV Wires, Three Prices

Most people who text us about a TV ask the same follow-up: what happens to the cords? You have three choices. An exterior cord cover (a slim paintable channel on the wall surface) adds $50 to a mount. In-wall concealment, where the cables run inside the wall and nothing shows, adds $175. Running wires through a fireplace wall adds $250.

If your TV is already mounted and you only want the dangling cords dealt with, that is a standalone visit and it starts at $165. Text us a photo of the wall and we will tell you which method fits and what your exact price is before you book.

Which One Is Right for You

Cord Cover vs In-Wall: How Each One Looks

Exterior cord cover (+$50)

A cord cover is a narrow plastic channel that sticks or screws to the wall and holds all the cables in one straight line from the bottom of the TV down to the outlet. From across the room it reads as a thin vertical strip, about the width of two fingers. It can be painted to match the wall, and once painted most people stop noticing it. It is the right choice for brick, stone, concrete and plaster, for renters who cannot cut into walls, and for anyone who wants a clean look at the lowest price. It is also the quickest option.

In-wall concealment (+$175)

This is the look in the photos: a TV on the wall with nothing hanging below it. We make an opening behind the TV and another low on the wall, usually behind your console, and run the cables through the wall cavity between them. It works on standard drywall with an open stud bay. Low-voltage cables like HDMI can run through the wall directly. A regular TV power cord is not rated to sit inside a wall, so power is handled with an in-wall rated power kit or an outlet behind the TV. If your job needs a kit, longer HDMI cables or other parts, we tell you the cost before the job.

In-wall is not always possible. Exterior walls full of insulation, horizontal fire blocking between studs, plaster and lath, and masonry can all get in the way. That is why we ask for a photo first. If in-wall will not work on your wall, we will say so and price the cord cover instead.

Through a fireplace wall (+$250)

A TV above a fireplace is the hardest place to hide wires, because the firebox, the flue and extra framing sit exactly where the cables want to go. On a framed and drywalled fireplace wall we can usually route cables around the firebox to a side cabinet or outlet. On solid brick or stone there is no cavity, so the answer is normally a cord cover run along a mortar line or the edge of the surround. See TV mounting on brick and stone for how that works.

Soundbar, Apple TV and cable box

Hiding the TV cords only helps if the boxes are dealt with too. A streaming device or small cable box can be mounted behind the TV for $90, and a soundbar mounted under the TV is $85. Tell us what plugs into the TV and we will plan one cable path for all of it.

TV Wire Concealment Questions

How much does it cost to hide TV wires? +
When added to a TV mount, an exterior cord cover is $50, in-wall concealment is $175, and routing wires through a fireplace wall is $250. If the TV is already mounted and you only need the wires hidden, a standalone visit starts at $165. All prices are plus 8% sales tax.
What is the difference between in-wall and an exterior cord cover? +
An exterior cord cover is a slim paintable channel on the surface of the wall that holds the cables in one straight line. You can see the strip, but not the cords. In-wall concealment runs the cables inside the wall so nothing is visible below the TV. The cord cover is $50 and works on any wall. In-wall is $175 and needs standard drywall with an open cavity.
Can you hide the wires on a TV that is already mounted? +
Yes. Wire concealment on its own visit starts at $165 plus tax. Text a photo of the TV and the wall below it to (475) 500-7126 and we will confirm the method and the exact price before you book.
Is it safe to put the TV power cord inside the wall? +
A regular TV power cord is not rated to run inside a wall. For in-wall concealment the HDMI and other low-voltage cables go through the wall, and power is handled with an in-wall rated power kit or an outlet behind the TV. Any kit or other parts your job needs are the client's responsibility, and we will tell you what is needed before the job.
Can you hide wires on a brick, stone or plaster wall? +
Yes, with an exterior cord cover. Solid masonry has no cavity to run cables through, and plaster and lath often cracks or blocks the path. A paintable cord cover run in a straight line, or along a mortar joint on brick, is the clean option on those walls.
Can you hide the soundbar, Apple TV or cable box too? +
Yes. A soundbar mounted under the TV is $85 and a streaming device or small cable box mounted behind the TV is $90. We plan one cable path so all of it is hidden together.
How long does wire concealment take? +
A cord cover adds very little time. In-wall concealment usually adds 30 to 60 minutes to a TV mount. Most TV mounts with wires hidden are done in 1 to 2 hours. Fireplace walls can take longer.
How do I pay? +
We're cashless. Your card is saved when you book, held 24 hours before the appointment, and charged only after the job is done. No deposit.
