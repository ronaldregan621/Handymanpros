What We Install and Maintain

The Six Modifications That Matter Most

These are the highest-impact safety upgrades for any Connecticut home where an older adult is living or will be living.

🚿

Grab Bar Installation

Properly anchored grab bars near toilets, in showers, and along tub walls. Installed into wall studs or with toggle anchors rated for 250 pounds. This is the single highest-impact modification for fall prevention in the bathroom. See full grab bar service ›

🪜

Handrail Reinforcement

Existing handrails checked for structural integrity and tightened or replaced. Exterior steps that lack a railing get one installed. Interior staircases get handrails on both sides where code allows and the physical layout permits.

🚪

Threshold Ramps

Raised door saddles are invisible trip hazards, especially for walkers and canes. Rubber or aluminum threshold ramps eliminate the step without requiring any structural modification to the doorway.

💡

Safety Lighting Upgrades

Hallways, staircases, and bathrooms need brighter, more reliable lighting as eyesight changes with age. LED replacements in all fixtures, motion-activated night lights in key areas, and exterior lighting over entry steps.

🔔

Detector Compliance

Smoke and CO detector battery replacement, end-of-life unit replacement, and a full coverage check against Connecticut code. We document every unit in the home: location, battery status, manufacture date, and condition.

🔧

Ongoing Home Maintenance

The regular work that becomes unsafe or unreachable over time. Bulb replacements, weather stripping, minor repairs, tightened hardware, and seasonal checks. Available on a recurring basis so nothing is deferred until it becomes a hazard.

Common Questions

What home modifications help seniors age in place safely? +
The highest-impact modifications are grab bars in bathrooms near toilets and in showers, reinforced handrails on all staircases, threshold ramps over raised door saddles, improved lighting in hallways and stairwells, lever-style door handles, and non-slip surfaces in bathrooms. These address the five most common fall locations in a home for adults over 65.
Do I need a contractor or a handyman for these modifications? +
Most aging in place modifications do not require a licensed contractor. Grab bar installation, handrail reinforcement, threshold ramp installation, lighting upgrades, and door hardware changes are all handyman-level work. Structural modifications like doorway widening require a contractor. Alliance Handyman Pros handles all non-structural modifications and advises on anything that requires licensed work.
How much do aging in place modifications cost in Connecticut? +
Basic safety modifications, grab bars, handrails, threshold ramps, and lighting, typically run $300 to $800 total depending on the number of locations. This compares to $5,000 to $15,000 or more per month for assisted living in Fairfield County CT. A single safety visit from Alliance Handyman Pros costs a fraction of one month in an assisted living facility.
Can you help if I live out of state but need to handle a parent's home in Connecticut? +
Yes. We work regularly with adult children who live outside Connecticut and need trusted, responsive service for a parent's home in Fairfield County. You can book online or call, describe the situation, and we coordinate directly with whoever has access to the home. We provide a full report of everything completed, emailed to the family contact.
Do you handle ongoing maintenance for seniors who live alone? +
Yes. Beyond safety modifications, we handle the regular home maintenance that becomes difficult or unsafe for older adults: replacing light bulbs in high fixtures, changing smoke and CO detector batteries, tightening loose handrails, and dozens of small tasks that pile up. Recurring service visits are available for families who want a consistent set of eyes on the home.
