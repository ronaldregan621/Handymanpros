## Three Ways to Partner

### 1. Designer Trade Account
For interior designers who need installs done right and on schedule.

- **Trade pricing** on labor across mounting, hanging, assembly, and finish work
- **Priority scheduling** ahead of general-public bookings
- **Single point of contact** who knows your projects and your standards
- **Consolidated invoicing** across multiple client jobs
- **Net-15 terms** so billing never holds up a reveal

### 2. Property and Commercial Account
For property managers and commercial operators who need a dependable maintenance partner.

- **Account pricing** with volume rates for recurring work
- **Priority scheduling** for turns, make-readies, and tenant requests
- **Single point of contact** for dispatch and follow-up
- **Consolidated invoicing** by property or portfolio
- **Net-30 terms** to fit your AP cycle

### 3. Realtor Preferred-Vendor Program
For realtors who need fast, reliable work to get listings ready and close deals.

- **Preferred pricing** on pre-listing repairs, punch lists, and post-inspection fixes
- **Priority scheduling** to hit listing and closing deadlines
- **Single point of contact** who understands deal timelines
- **Reciprocal referral relationship** — we send your name to our homeowner clients who are buying or selling, and you send ours. This is a RESPA-safe referral of mutual goodwill. **No cash kickbacks, no referral fees, no pay-per-lead.** Just two trusted local pros looking out for the same clients.
