FAQ

Common Questions

If yours isn't here, just call. We're easy to reach.

Do you work with condo and HOA associations? +
Yes. Condo and HOA associations are one of our core commercial relationships, including waterfront condo associations and older historic buildings that call for a careful hand. We handle common area lighting, hallway and stairwell fixtures, detector compliance, and recurring punch lists on a scheduled basis, with one point of contact for the board or property manager and a single consolidated invoice.
Can one vendor cover many small jobs across multiple buildings? +
That is exactly the model. Instead of calling a different trade for every bulb, loose handrail, sticking door, or detector battery across your portfolio, you batch the list and we clear it on a scheduled visit. One point of contact, one service record format for every building, and one monthly invoice covering all locations.
Do you offer consolidated invoicing and net-30 terms? +
Yes. For property managers and associations running multiple buildings, we consolidate every location onto a single monthly invoice with net-30 terms. Each line shows the building, the work performed, and the date, so the documentation drops straight into your building files and board reporting.
Do you handle fire and smoke detector compliance documentation? +
Yes. We test smoke and CO detectors, replace batteries and end of life units, and confirm coverage against Connecticut code, then provide a written record of every unit by location, battery status, manufacture date, and condition. That record satisfies your annual testing documentation requirement and goes on file for the fire marshal and your insurer.
How fast do you respond between scheduled visits? +
Scheduled accounts get priority. Most days we have same day and next day availability for the items that cannot wait for the next planned visit: a dark stairwell, a failed entry door closer, a turnover unit that has to show tomorrow. You call one number and a crew member who already knows your building responds.
What is a building walkthrough and does it cost anything? +
It is a no charge visit where we walk your property or portfolio with you, log the recurring tasks, note ceiling heights and access requirements, and quote a scheduled maintenance plan in writing before any work begins. Call (475) 500-7126 or email alliancehandymanpros@gmail.com with the subject "Commercial walkthrough" to set one up.
