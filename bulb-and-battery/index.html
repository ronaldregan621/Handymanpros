Connecticut Code

What CT Requires and What We See

Most homes in Fairfield County are under-protected. We see it on every visit.

10 yrs

Detector Lifespan

Smoke detectors expire after 8 to 10 years regardless of whether they still chirp when tested. Sensors degrade. We check manufacture dates on every visit.

6+

Detectors for a 3BR Home

CT requires one per level, one inside each bedroom, and one outside each sleeping area. Most homes we walk into have 2 to 3. That's not enough.

100%

Homes with CO Exposure

Any home with a gas appliance, attached garage, or fireplace requires CO detection. Older homes frequently have gaps. We find and fix them.

FAQ

Common Questions

If yours isn't here, just call. We answer fast.

Can you replace bulbs in vaulted or high ceilings? +
Yes, that's one of the most common reasons people call us. We carry the right ladders and extension equipment for recessed cans, vaulted ceiling pendants, foyer chandeliers, and two-story great rooms. No rental, no risk on your end.
My smoke detector is still chirping after I replaced the battery. What's happening? +
Usually a residual charge issue (most common), bad battery contact, an end-of-life unit, or a different unit than the one you replaced. We diagnose on the spot and fix it: either reset, swap the battery correctly, or replace the unit. We carry Kidde and First Alert on the truck.
How many smoke detectors does my Connecticut home need? +
At minimum: one on every level (including basement), one inside each bedroom, and one outside each sleeping area. A 3-bedroom home needs at least 6. We do compliance walk-throughs and can bring the home fully up to CT code in a single visit.
Do you replace the whole detector or just the battery? +
Both, whatever the unit needs. If it's a battery issue, we replace the battery and reset. If the unit is past its 8 to 10 year lifespan or not responding correctly, we replace the whole unit. We carry the most common residential units on the truck.
