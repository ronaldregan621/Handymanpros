What We Assemble

Every IKEA Product Line, Done Correctly

IKEA uses a cam lock and dowel system that is efficient when assembled in the right sequence. Out-of-order steps and overtightened cam locks are the two most common reasons DIY assemblies fail. We have done each of these products dozens of times.

Most Requested

PAX Wardrobe Systems

Multi-frame configurations with KOMPLEMENT interior organizers, soft-close hinges, and sliding or hinged door systems. Proper sequencing and wall anchoring required. 3 to 5 hours for a full two-frame system.

■

KALLAX Shelving Units

4x4 and larger configurations with 32 or more cam locks. Squareness before the back panel is non-negotiable. Inserts, doors, and casters added at completion. Popular for home offices and living rooms.

🏠

HEMNES Bedroom Furniture

Solid pine construction with dovetail drawer assembly. Drawer runner alignment determines how smoothly everything operates for years. Dressers, bedside tables, TV stands, and storage benches.

🌑

MALM Beds and Dressers

Bed frames with slatted bases, headboard attachment, and optional under-bed storage drawers. Dressers with multiple cam lock sequences per drawer. All tall MALM pieces anchored to wall studs per CPSC guidelines.

📺

BESTA Storage and TV Units

Modular frame systems that must be leveled and anchored before doors are hung. Push-open mechanisms, soft-close hinges, and glass door variants. Frequently combined with floating top panels.

📚

BILLY Bookcases and Extensions

Individual BILLY units are straightforward. Multi-unit configurations with extension units, bridges, and glass door attachments require sequencing and leveling across the full run. We handle both.

What IKEA furniture do you assemble? +
We assemble the full IKEA catalog including PAX wardrobe systems with KOMPLEMENT organizers, KALLAX shelving units, HEMNES dressers and storage, MALM bed frames and chest of drawers, BESTA TV units and cabinets, BILLY bookcases with extensions and bridges, STUVA and SMASTAD children's storage, and all other IKEA flat-pack products.
Do you anchor IKEA furniture to the wall? +
Yes, wall anchoring is included with every assembly. IKEA ships anti-tip straps with PAX wardrobes, HEMNES dressers, MALM chests, and BILLY bookcases. We anchor all tall pieces to wall studs as a standard part of the job. In homes with plaster walls or stud spacing that does not align, we use the appropriate toggle anchors rated for the wall type.
Can you assemble IKEA furniture I already started? +
In most cases, yes. The most common issue in partially assembled IKEA furniture is an overtightened cam lock that has stripped the particle board bore. This can often be salvaged with a repair insert and re-drilling. We assess the condition when we arrive and let you know before proceeding if a component needs replacement.
