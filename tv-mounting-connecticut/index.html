What Goes Wrong

Four Mistakes That End With a Broken TV or a Call to Us

Most TV mounting problems are not about tools or effort. They are about decisions made before the first drill hole. Here is what we see and fix on a regular basis in Fairfield County.

Problem 01

TV Mounted Too High

Above a standard fireplace mantel, your viewing angle is 35 to 45 degrees above eye level. THX and SMPTE define comfortable viewing at 0 to 15 degrees above eye level. Extended watching at 35 degrees causes neck strain, eye fatigue, and eventually a TV you stop using as your primary screen.

35 to 45 degrees above eye level = discomfort per THX and SMPTE standards
Problem 02

TV Pulled From the Wall

Drywall screws hold 5 to 10 lbs each without studs. A 65-inch TV weighs 55 to 70 lbs. A full-motion mount extended 18 inches multiplies the effective load by a factor of three or more. Wrong anchors do not fail immediately. They loosen over weeks and fail at the worst moment.

5 to 10 lbs per drywall screw vs. 55 to 90 lbs for a large TV
Problem 03

Heat Damage Above a Gas Fireplace

A gas fireplace produces 20,000 to 35,000 BTUs per hour. That is enough heat to warm a 1,400-square-foot home. Extended exposure to those temperatures causes dead pixels, melted components, and a voided manufacturer warranty. Tilt mounts and correct clearance are required, not optional.

20,000 to 35,000 BTUs per hour from a standard gas fireplace
Problem 04

Samsung Frame Installed With the Wrong Mount

The Frame's outer VESA holes are recessed and designed for the Samsung No Gap Wall Mount only. Standard mounts attached to those holes leave the TV sitting an inch off the wall. The one-connect cable does not route correctly. The magnetic contacts do not engage. We fix this regularly.

Inner VESA holes only with Slim Fit or micro-gap mount

TV Mounting Questions

How much does TV mounting cost in Connecticut? +
Our flat rate starts at $199 for standard mounts on TVs 55 inches and smaller. Larger TVs start at $239. Above-fireplace installs and Samsung Frame installations start at $249. Wire concealment is quoted by scope. No hourly billing and no charges added after the fact. Call (475) 500-7126 to confirm your specific job before booking.
Do you mount TVs above fireplaces in Fairfield County? +
Yes. Above-fireplace TV mounting is among our most common requests. We use tilt mounts that bring the viewing angle down toward a comfortable range, address heat clearance requirements, and handle wire concealment through or around the fireplace surround. We also advise on electric vs. gas fireplace heat output and what clearance is needed for your specific TV model.
Can you mount a TV on plaster walls in Connecticut? +
Yes. Plaster and lath walls are extremely common in Greenwich, Darien, New Canaan, Westport, and Wilton, where a significant portion of homes were built before 1960. Plaster alone will not hold a TV, but studs behind the plaster absolutely will. We locate them correctly using a multi-step process and use the appropriate non-pointed hardware for plaster walls. We can also use a plywood backing plate method when stud spacing does not match your TV's VESA hole pattern.
Do you install Samsung Frame TVs? +
Yes. The Frame is one of our most common TV mounting requests across Fairfield County. It requires inner VESA holes (not the outer recessed holes), a Slim Fit or micro-gap compatible mount with a 7.5mm or smaller profile, and correct engagement of the magnetic contacts. We also handle one-connect cable routing so the install looks clean and intentional from every angle.
Do you hide the wires after mounting? +
Yes. We offer two wire concealment approaches. For drywall walls, we run cables inside the wall using a UL-listed in-wall power kit for the power cable, which meets electrical code. For plaster, brick, or concrete walls where in-wall is not feasible, we use a paintable raceway that runs along the wall surface and can be painted to match your wall color.
Do you provide the wall mount bracket? +
The base price does not include the mount. Many customers already have the mount they want. If you need us to source a mount, we can recommend the correct type for your wall and TV combination. Samsung Frame mounts, full-motion mounts for plaster walls, and fireplace tilt mounts all have specific requirements we can guide you through. Call (475) 500-7126 to discuss before booking.
