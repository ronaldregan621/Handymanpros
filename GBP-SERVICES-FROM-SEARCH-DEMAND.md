# GBP Services From Search Demand — Alliance Handyman Pros

**The move (2 minutes, moves map pack ranking in days):** Google matches a searcher's query against your **service names and descriptions**. Naming a service after a phrase people actually search is a direct relevance signal. This adds services built from **proven demand** (the queries you already surface for, plus the ones competitors currently win that you don't).

## Do this
1. Open **business.google.com → Performance → How people discovered you → search terms.** Read the real keywords, ranked by volume.
2. Open **Edit services** on the profile.
3. Add each service below whose keyword matches a term in your Performance list (or an obvious core service you provide). Paste the description as-is.
4. Prioritize by *your* Performance volume. The drafts below are ordered by proven opportunity from our AI-citation baseline (the services where you are invisible today are the biggest wins).

**Rules:** only real services you perform, keyword + a town in every description, one clean service per phrase, no keyword stuffing, no dashes.
