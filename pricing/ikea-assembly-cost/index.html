What Affects Your Quote

What Goes Into an IKEA Assembly Quote

A flat rate is not a guess. When we quote your price before you book, these are the things that move it up or down. The same factors drive an hourly bill, the difference is that with us they are settled upfront.

💼

Piece Complexity

A single BILLY bookcase is a quick build. A full PAX wardrobe with KOMPLEMENT organizers, soft-close hinges, and sliding doors is a 3 to 5 hour job. The more cam locks, doors, and interior fittings a piece has, the more it weighs on the quote.

📦

Number of Items

One dresser is different from a full bedroom of a MALM bed, two HEMNES nightstands, and a chest of drawers. We look at everything on the list together and quote the whole visit as one flat rate rather than metering each box.

🔧

Wall Anchoring

Tall pieces like PAX, HEMNES, and MALM need to be anchored to the wall so they cannot tip. Anchoring is included in the flat rate. Plaster walls or stud spacing that does not line up can call for specific toggle anchors, which we factor in when we quote.

💳

Delivery Condition

Flat packs that arrived complete and undamaged go together cleanly. Missing hardware, a crushed box, or a piece someone already started with a stripped cam lock adds work. We assess condition on arrival and flag anything before we proceed.
