The Honest Answer

Flat Rate You Approve, Not a Meter You Watch.

The most useful thing to understand about furniture assembly pricing in Fairfield County is the difference between two billing models. One is hourly: the clock starts when the technician walks in and keeps running until the last cam lock is tightened. The other is flat rate: the full price is set and agreed before anyone touches a box.

Alliance Handyman Pros charges a flat rate per visit that covers the full job regardless of how long it takes, and we quote that exact rate before you book. If a bed frame fights back, if a PAX wardrobe has 200 parts, if the instructions are wrong and we have to back a step out, the price you agreed to does not move. That is the whole point of flat rate. You approve one number and that is the number you pay.

Hourly assembly services in Fairfield County work differently. They commonly start at $52 or more per hour, and a complex multi piece job climbs to $156 to $260 or more once the meter runs across several hours. The slower the build, the more you pay, which is backwards from how most people would want to be charged.

What Goes Into the Quote

What Affects a Furniture Assembly Quote

When we set your flat rate before booking, these are the things we look at. Understanding them helps you see why two jobs are not always priced the same, and why the number is fair either way.

🔢

Number of Pieces

One nightstand is not a full bedroom set. The more items on the list, the more work involved, and that factors into the flat rate we quote up front so a big delivery day never becomes a surprise.

🧩

Piece Complexity

A simple console with four legs is quick. A PAX wardrobe wall, a storage platform bed, or a modular sectional has far more parts and steps. Complexity is priced into the quote, not billed by the minute.

🔩

Wall Anchoring on Tall Items

Bookshelves, wardrobes, and dressers get strapped to the wall studs as standard. IKEA ships anti tip hardware with most tall furniture and we install it. It is part of the job, not an add on charge.

🪑

Brand and Material

A heavy solid wood Restoration Hardware piece is a different build than a lightweight flat pack console. RH, Pottery Barn, West Elm, Crate and Barrel, Article, CB2, Wayfair, and IKEA all vary in weight and part count, and that shows up in the quote.

📦

Delivery Condition

Furniture that arrived complete and in good boxes goes together cleanly. Missing hardware, damaged panels, or a half started build you got stuck on can add steps, and we account for the real scope when we quote you.

📍
