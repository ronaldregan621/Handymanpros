What Affects the Price

Five Things That Move Your TV Mounting Cost

The base numbers above are flat and confirmed before we start. These are the factors that decide which number your job lands on.

📺

TV Size

A TV 55 inches and smaller is a flat $199. Larger TVs over 55 inches start at $239 because they usually need a two person lift and heavier duty hardware to stay secure and level.

🧱

Wall Type

Drywall with wood studs is the standard install. Plaster and lath in pre 1960 homes and metal studs in newer condos each need a different anchor approach, which can affect the time and the scope.

🔥

Above the Fireplace Masonry

An above the fireplace install starts at $249. Brick and stone surrounds need masonry drilling and sleeve anchors plus correct heat clearance, so a masonry fireplace mount sits toward the higher end.

🔒

Wire Concealment Scope

Hiding the wires is quoted by scope. In wall routing on drywall with a UL listed power kit is one price, paintable raceway on plaster or brick is another. The length of the run matters too.

↻

Mount Type

A fixed or tilt bracket is the standard job. Full motion and articulating arms carry more load and need careful stud anchoring, and a Samsung Frame needs a specific slim mount, both of which change the price.

💰

Whether You Bring the Mount

The bracket is not included in the base price. If you already own the right mount, that is all you pay for. If you need us to source it, we add the cost of the correct bracket and confirm the total first.
