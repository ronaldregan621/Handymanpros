What Affects the Price

Five Things That Move Your TV Mounting Cost

The base numbers above are confirmed before we start. These are the factors that decide which number your job lands on.

📺

TV Size

TVs up to 65 inches on drywall are $199. TVs 66 to 70 inches are $200 and 86 inches and larger are $385, because bigger panels need a two person lift and more time to stay secure and level.

🧱

Wall Type

Drywall with wood studs is the standard install. Plaster and lath in pre 1960 homes and metal studs in newer condos each need a different anchor approach. Indoor brick, stone or concrete adds $120.

🔥

Above the Fireplace Masonry

An above the fireplace install starts at $250. Brick and stone surrounds need masonry drilling and sleeve anchors plus correct heat clearance, which adds $120.

🔒

Wire Concealment Scope

Hiding the wires is a fixed add-on. An exterior cord cover is $50, in wall concealment on drywall is $175, and routing through a fireplace wall is $250.

↻

Mount Type

A fixed or tilt bracket is the standard job. Full motion and articulating arms carry more load and need careful stud anchoring, and a Samsung Frame needs a specific slim mount, both of which change the price.

💰

Whether You Bring the Mount

The bracket is not included in the base price. If you already own the right mount, we use it. If you need one, we bring a quality mount for $85.
