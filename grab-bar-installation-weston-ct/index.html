Bathroom Safety in Weston

Grab Bars That Hold in Older Weston Homes

The bathroom is where most falls at home happen, and the CDC reports that falls are the leading cause of injury among adults over 65. A grab bar changes that math, but only if it is anchored to carry real body weight. In Weston that anchoring question is the whole job, because the town's large custom colonials and older farmhouses on wooded lots were often built to individual specs rather than a standard framing plan. A shower wall here might be tile over a mortar bed, a fiberglass surround, or plaster over lath, and each one calls for a different approach.

We locate the stud behind the wall first and anchor directly into it whenever the ideal bar position lines up. When it does not, we set rated backing or use toggle anchors rated for 300 lbs per anchor, two per bar end. On the plaster over lath walls found in the older Lyons Plains and Weston Center homes, a hollow anchor alone will not hold, so we add backing behind the surface. AARP recommends professional installation for exactly this reason: a bar that feels solid under a gentle push can still pull out under a full grab during a fall.

Shower Grab Bars

Horizontal bars along the shower wall for balance, typically 24 to 36 inches at 33 to 36 inch ADA height. Anchored through tile or fiberglass surrounds into the stud or rated backing behind them.

Tub Entry Bars

Vertical or diagonal bars at the tub entry, the single most hazardous movement in any Weston bathroom. Turns a one legged step over the tub wall into a supported transfer.

Toilet Side Bars

Bars beside the toilet to support lowering and rising. Offset flange mounting available where a stud does not sit exactly where the bar needs to go.

Tile and Plaster Walls

Tile drilled with carbide tip bits at low speed, no cracks. Plaster over lath backed with a solid plate so the bar carries the same load as a stud anchored install.

Fold Down Bars

For smaller bathrooms where a fixed bar would block access. Folds flat against the wall when not in use, common in guest and second floor baths.

Can you anchor grab bars into tile?
Yes. Many Weston bathrooms have tile or fiberglass surrounds. We drill tile with carbide tip bits at low speed, then anchor into the stud behind the surround or into rated backing where a stud is not aligned. A tile surcharge of $99 to $129 per bar applies. We have not cracked a tile on a properly executed install.
Can you install grab bars in Weston the same week?

What height should a grab bar be installed at?
ADA guidance places horizontal shower, tub, and toilet side bars 33 to 36 inches above the floor, measured to the top of the gripping surface. We confirm the exact height with the person who will actually use the bar on site.
Can you install a grab bar I already bought?
Yes. If you already purchased a Moen, Delta, Kohler, or other bar, we install it at the single bar labor rate of $235 and confirm it meets the ADA 250 lb load standard before mounting. Supplying your own bar lowers the price on every package.
