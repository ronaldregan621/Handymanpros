Common Questions

If yours isn't here, just call. We're easy to reach.

Do you bring your own tools? +
Yes. We bring everything: proper hex drivers, a drill set to the right clutch so we don't strip the cam locks, a level, and anything else the job needs. You don't need to supply a single thing.
Can you assemble furniture I already started and got stuck on? +
Absolutely. We see this all the time. If you're halfway through and stuck, or if something's been assembled wrong, we take over and fix it. No judgment. It's one of the most common reasons people call us.
What brands do you assemble? +
All of them. IKEA, Wayfair, Amazon, Ashley Furniture, Pottery Barn, Restoration Hardware, West Elm, Crate & Barrel, Article, and anything else that ships flat-packed or in multiple boxes.
Do you handle large pieces like PAX wardrobes and sectional sofas? +
Yes, and large pieces are actually where we add the most value. PAX wardrobes, full sectionals, king-size bed frames with storage, entertainment wall units: all within scope.
Do you wall-anchor tall furniture? +
Yes, always. We anchor every tall piece (bookshelves, wardrobes, dressers) to wall studs. Unanchored tall furniture is a real safety hazard, especially in homes with young children. It's not an upsell. It's how we do every job.
