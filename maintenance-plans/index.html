The Annual Inspection

The Visit That Pays for the Plan

Once a year we walk the entire home and check the things that fail quietly, the ones that turn into the expensive call when nobody is watching. You get a written report: what needs attention now, what to keep an eye on, and a clear price on anything you want us to handle. A failed door seal is a caulk line today and a rot repair next year. The inspection is how we get ahead of it.

Smoke and CO detectors tested, dated, and replaced where needed, plus battery checks on high fixtures handled the same way our bulb and battery service does.
Caulk and grout lines in every bath and the kitchen, exterior trim, and door and window seals checked for the gaps that let water in.
Railings, banisters, and exterior steps tested for movement, with reinforcement that overlaps our aging-in-place safety work when an older adult lives in the home.
GFCI outlets, visible plumbing, water heater area, and the small fixes on your running list, including any TV mounting or mounting jobs you have been meaning to book.

FAQ

Common Questions

If yours isn't here, just call. We're easy to reach.

What is included in a home maintenance plan? +
Every plan includes priority scheduling so members get the next open slot ahead of one-off calls, a standing labor discount on every visit, and an annual home safety and systems inspection. The inspection is a full walk-through of the things that fail quietly: smoke and CO detectors, caulk and grout lines, exterior trim and door seals, loose railings, GFCI outlets, and high fixtures. You receive a written report with what needs attention now and what to watch.
How does the second-home watch service work? +
For owners whose Fairfield County house sits empty for stretches, we run scheduled property check-ins on a set cadence, walk the home after major storms, and meet your other vendors at the door so the plumber, the alarm tech, or the cleaning team can get in while you are away. You get one point of contact who knows the house, and a short report after each visit. It is the difference between hoping nothing happened and knowing someone walked the property.
Why pay for a plan instead of calling when something breaks? +
Two reasons. First, members pay a discounted labor rate and get scheduling priority, so a plan usually costs less over a year than paying full price for every separate call. Second, the annual inspection catches the small problems before they become the expensive ones. A failed door seal is a caulk line today and a rot repair next year. The plan keeps a trusted, insured local crew already familiar with your home, instead of starting from scratch with a stranger each time.

What is a prepaid hours-bank and how is it different from a membership? +
A membership is an annual relationship with set benefits and a yearly inspection. A prepaid hours-bank is the simpler alternative: you buy a block of labor hours up front at a member rate and draw them down across the year as work comes up, with the same priority scheduling. Some owners prefer the predictability of a membership, others prefer the flexibility of a bank. We can set up either, or move you between them at renewal.
