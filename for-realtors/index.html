Agent Questions

Do you have a flat price package for pre listing punch lists? +
Yes. Our Listing Ready package is a flat quoted pre photo punch list: patch and touch up the nail holes and scuffs, hang and level mirrors and art, swap dated or broken hardware, fix the sticky door and the loose railing, and clear the small stuff buyers fixate on in photos and at showings. You get one quoted price up front with no surprise charges, so you know the number before you commit the seller.
Can you turn work around before my listing photos or my closing date? +
That is the whole point of the program. We hold same day and next day slots for agent accounts and we work to your photo date and your closing date, not a four hour window. Tell us when the photographer is coming or when the buyer takes possession and we schedule the crew to finish before then.
How does the preferred vendor relationship work, and is it RESPA safe? +
You add us to your trusted vendor sheet and we recommend you to our own clients who are buying or selling. It is a reciprocal referral relationship built on doing good work, not a kickback. There is no cash paid for referrals in either direction, which keeps it clean under RESPA. We also give you a co branded leave behind you can hand clients so the recommendation comes from you and reflects on you.
What is included in the Move In Ready package after closing? +
Move In Ready is the post close visit that makes a new owner feel settled on day one: mount the TVs, assemble the furniture that arrived in boxes, childproof for families with young kids, install grab bars for older buyers, and handle the hardware items like changing the locks and swapping fixtures. It is the closing gift that actually gets used, and it is the work new owners are going to need a handyman for anyway.
Do you offer net terms or billing for agent accounts? +
Yes. Agent and brokerage accounts can be set up on net terms so you are not paying out of pocket at the door on every job. We can bill the agent, the seller, or the buyer depending on how the deal is structured, and we send a clean itemized invoice for your file. Set the account up once and every job after that is a single call or email.
What towns do you cover for real estate agents? +
All of Fairfield County CT including Greenwich, Darien, New Canaan, Westport, Wilton, Stamford, and Norwalk, plus nearby Westchester County NY. If you have a listing or a closing just outside that range, call (475) 500-7126 and we will tell you right away whether we can cover it.
