Pricing

How much does TV mounting cost? +
Mounting one TV up to 65 inches on drywall starts at $199. Above a fireplace starts at $250, a Samsung Frame TV at $400, and an outdoor TV at $540. Larger TVs, brick or stone walls, and extras like hiding wires add to that. You get an itemized total, tax included, before anything is booked. Text us a photo of the wall and we'll send your price, usually within 15 minutes.
Is sales tax included in your prices? +
No. Listed prices are before 8% sales tax. Every quote we send shows the tax line and the final total, so the number you agree to is the number you pay.
Will there be any extra charges after the job? +
Standard screws, anchors and mounting hardware are included. If your wall or setup needs anything beyond that, we tell you the cost and get your OK before we use it.
Do you charge by the hour? +
No. TV mounting and our listed services are flat prices. Small handyman jobs start at $165.
Can I get a price without an in-person visit? +
Yes. Text a photo of the wall and tell us the TV size, the wall type and any extras (hiding wires, soundbar, devices). We send an itemized price by text, usually within 15 minutes during business hours.

The mount and the TV

Is the TV mount included? +
No. The base price assumes you already have a mount. If you need one, we can bring a mount sized for your TV for $85.
What size TVs do you mount? +
All sizes. Up to 65 inches is the base price. 66 to 85 inches adds $65, and 86 inches or larger adds $250.
Can you mount more than one TV in the same visit? +
Yes. Each additional TV on the same visit is $150.
Can you mount my soundbar, Apple TV or cable box? +
Yes. Soundbar mounting is $85. Mounting a streaming box, cable box or Sonos device behind the TV is $90 per device.
Do you mount Samsung Frame TVs? +
Yes, from $400. That includes flush mounting and placing the One Connect box. Hiding the One Connect box in a cabinet or inside the wall costs extra.

Walls and fireplaces

Can you mount a TV on brick, stone or concrete? +
Yes. We drill with masonry bits and use masonry anchors. Brick, stone and concrete walls add $120.
Can you mount on plaster walls? +
Yes. Plaster and lath are common in older homes. Send us a photo and we'll confirm the price before booking.
Can you mount a TV above a fireplace? +
Yes, from $250 on a drywall chimney, plus $120 if it's brick or stone. We'll recommend a viewing height. If heat from the fireplace is a concern for your TV, we'll tell you before we start.

Hiding wires

Can you hide the TV wires? +
Yes, two ways:
  • Exterior cord cover ($50): a slim, paintable channel on the wall surface. Quick, and works on any wall.
  • In-wall concealment ($175): cables run inside the wall to a low plate, so nothing shows. Needs a drywall cavity without fire blocking.
Running wires through a fireplace surround is $250. Wire hiding on its own, without a new mount, starts at $165.
Can you hide wires on a brick wall? +
Usually with an exterior cord cover, since brick can't be opened like drywall. Send a photo and we'll tell you what's possible.
Can you add an outlet behind the TV? +
Send us a photo. Some setups need an electrician, and we'll tell you before you book.

Other TV work

Can you take down, move or remount a TV? +
Yes. Take-down, moving a TV to a mount that's already up, and unboxing and setup start at $165. Mounting a TV on a new wall is priced like a new install.
Do you mount TVs outdoors? +
Yes, from $540, with weather-rated hardware. Brick or concrete exterior walls add $75.

Booking and scheduling

How soon can you come? +
Often the next day. When you text or call, we offer the next two open times right away.
How long does it take? +
Most single-TV installs take 1 to 2 hours. Hiding wires in the wall or mounting on brick adds time.
How do I book? +
Book online, call, or text (475) 500-7126. If you agree to a price by text or phone, we set up the booking for you and send a confirmation link.
What areas do you serve? +
Fairfield County, Connecticut and Westchester County, New York, including Greenwich, Stamford, Darien, New Canaan, Norwalk, Westport, Rye, Harrison, Scarsdale and White Plains. Outside those? Text us your town and we'll confirm.

Payment and policies

How do I pay? +
We're cashless. Your card is saved when you book, held 24 hours before the appointment, and charged only after the job is done. No deposit.
Do you take Zelle, checks or cash? +
No. We take credit and debit cards only.
What is your cancellation policy? +
Cancel or reschedule free with at least 24 hours' notice. Cancellations under 24 hours may have a $75 fee.
Are you insured? +
Yes, we carry general liability insurance. If your building needs a certificate of insurance (COI) before work, tell us when you book and we'll send it.
Do you bring your own tools? +
Yes. Our technician brings all tools and standard hardware.
