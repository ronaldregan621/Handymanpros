# Alliance Handyman Pros

## Who We Are
Alliance Handyman Pros is a local install and mounting specialist serving Fairfield County, Connecticut and lower Westchester County, New York. We are owner operated, not a franchise and not a tech support aggregator. We know the local housing stock: plaster and lath walls in older colonials, stone and brick fireplaces, tile and fiberglass bathrooms, and high rise condos.

## Services
- **TV Mounting**: standard wall mounts on drywall, brick, stone, and above the fireplace. Starts at $199 for TVs 55 inches and smaller. Larger TVs start at $239. Above the fireplace installs start at $249.
- **Samsung Frame Installation**: no gap mounting, One Connect cable routing, wire concealment. Starts at $249.
- **Wire and Cable Concealment**: in wall routing or paintable raceways, quoted by scope.
- **Furniture Assembly**: IKEA, Wayfair, West Elm, Pottery Barn, Restoration Hardware, Crate and Barrel, Article, and CB2. Flat rate per visit, wall anchoring included on tall pieces.
- **IKEA Assembly**: PAX, KALLAX, HEMNES, MALM, BESTA, BILLY and the full catalog. Flat rate per visit, not hourly.
- **Grab Bar Installation**: anchored into studs or solid backing at ADA height, including tile and fiberglass surrounds. Starts at $235 for a single bar. Packages run up to $850 for six bars.
- **Aging in Place**: grab bars, handrails, and threshold ramps so older adults can stay safe at home.
- **Childproofing and Baby Proofing**: furniture anchoring, baby gates, cabinet and outlet locks, corner guards. Starts at $199.
- **Bulb and Battery Service**: high ceiling light bulb replacement and smoke detector battery and unit replacement, residential and commercial.

## Pricing
Flat and upfront, quoted before the job. No hourly billing and no charges added after the fact. Detailed cost guides are published at alliancehandymanpros.com/pricing for TV mounting, grab bar installation, IKEA assembly, and furniture assembly.
