Every IKEA Line, Built Correctly in Rye

The IKEA cam lock and dowel system requires assembly in a specific sequence. Out of order steps and overtightened cam locks are the two most common failure modes. We have built each of these dozens of times. See the full IKEA assembly service page for more detail.

PAX Wardrobe Systems

Single and multi frame systems with KOMPLEMENT organizers, soft close hinges, and sliding or hinged doors. 3 to 5 hours for a full system.

KALLAX Shelving

4x4 and larger units with 32 or more cam locks. Squareness before the back panel is critical. Inserts and door attachments at completion.

HEMNES Furniture

Solid pine dressers, bedside tables, and storage benches. Dovetail drawer assembly and precise runner alignment for smooth operation.

MALM Beds and Dressers

Bed frames with slatted bases and headboard attachment. All tall MALM pieces anchored per CPSC guidelines after the 29 million unit recall.

BESTA Storage Units

Modular frames leveled and anchored before doors are hung. Push open mechanisms, soft close hinges, and glass door variants.

BILLY Bookcases

Individual units and multi unit configurations with extension units, bridges, and glass doors. Leveled across the full run.
