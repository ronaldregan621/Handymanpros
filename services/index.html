Common Questions

How much does TV mounting cost? +
TV mounting on drywall is $199 plus tax for TVs up to 65 inches. TVs 66 to 70 inches are $200 and 86 inches and larger are $385. Above the fireplace starts at $250, a Samsung Frame starts at $400, and an outdoor TV starts at $540. The small job minimum for other handyman work is $165. Your price is confirmed before we start.
Is the TV mount included? +
No. The price covers the install and standard mounting hardware. If you have a mount, we use it. If you need one, we bring a quality mount for $85.
How much does it cost to hide the wires? +
An exterior cord cover is $50. In-wall wire concealment is $175. Routing the wires through a fireplace wall is $250. These are add-ons to a TV mount. See wire concealment.
How do I pay? Is there a deposit? +
We are a cashless company. There is no deposit. Your card is pre-authorized 24 hours before your appointment and charged once the job is complete.
What is your cancellation policy? +
Cancellations made less than 24 hours before your appointment may incur a $75 fee. To cancel or reschedule, call or text (475) 500-7126.
Do your prices include tax? +
No. All prices are plus 8% sales tax. A $199 TV mount comes to $214.92 with tax. The booking page shows the tax and total before you confirm.
How long does a TV install take? +
Most TV installs take 1 to 2 hours, depending on the TV size and the add-ons you choose.
Can you take down, move or remount a TV? +
Yes. We take down, move and remount TVs. Text us a photo of the current setup and the new spot at (475) 500-7126 and we will text back a price before you book. See TV remounting and relocation.
Are you insured? Can I get a certificate of insurance? +
Yes, we are insured. A certificate of insurance (COI) for your building or property manager is available on request. Ask when you book and tell us who the certificate holder is.
What areas do you serve? +
Fairfield County, CT and lower Westchester County, NY, including Greenwich, Stamford, Darien, New Canaan, Norwalk, Westport, Rye, Scarsdale and White Plains. Not sure? Text your ZIP code to (475) 500-7126.
