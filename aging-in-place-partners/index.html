Full Bathroom Safety

Provider Questions

How fast can you install for a hospital discharge? +
We hold same day and next day slots specifically for discharge cases. When you give us a discharge date, we schedule the install to land before the patient comes home so the bathroom, stairs, and entry are ready on day one. For multiple bar or full bathroom jobs we confirm the scope and the appointment by phone the same day you refer.
Do you provide documentation of the completed work for the care file? +
Yes. Every job comes with written documentation and before and after photos of each modification: bar locations, anchoring method, mounting height, and the finished install. We email the summary to you and the family so it can go straight into the patient's care file or the home health record. This is standard on every visit, not an add on.
Are your grab bars anchored to actually hold weight? +
Always. Bars are anchored into wall studs or into solid blocking, never into tile or drywall alone. When there is no stud where the patient needs the bar, we install proper backing rated for transfer loads. We do single bars, multiple bar layouts, and full bathroom safety packages including tub, shower, and toilet area placement that matches the patient's transfer pattern.
Can we set up a provider account and a direct line for referrals? +
Yes. We set up a provider account for your practice or agency with a direct line so your team is not routed through general intake. You send the referral, we coordinate scheduling and access with the family, and we report back when the work is done. Direct billing options are available so the modification does not have to come out of the patient's pocket at the door.
Are you insured, and do you follow aging in place best practices? +
We are fully insured and our team works to CAPS aligned aging in place and fall risk reduction best practices. That means correct bar heights and placement for the individual, threshold and stair fixes that remove trip hazards, and lighting and approach considerations, not just bars screwed to a wall. You can refer a client knowing the install will be done to a safety standard you can stand behind.
What towns do you cover for care provider referrals? +
All of Fairfield County CT including Greenwich, Darien, New Canaan, Westport, Wilton, Stamford, and Norwalk, plus nearby Westchester County NY. If you have a patient just outside that range, call (475) 500-7126 and we will tell you right away whether we can cover it. We also handle childproofing and home safety for multigenerational households on the same visit.
