What We Assemble

Office Furniture Assembly and Installation: Chairs, Desks, Workstations and Everything Around Them

If it arrived on a pallet or in a stack of boxes and has to be standing by Monday, it is on this list.

Office Chair Assembly

Task chairs, ergonomic chairs, mesh chairs, executive chairs, conference and guest seating. We seat the gas lift, torque the base, attach the arms and back, then set the tilt tension and seat height so each chair is ready for the person sitting in it.

  • Ten chairs or forty, done in one visit
  • Casters swapped for hard floor or carpet
  • Mismatched or missing hardware flagged before we start

Standing Desk Assembly

Motorized sit stand desks are the most common thing we build for offices right now. We assemble the frame, mount the top square, install the control box and keypad, route the power and monitor cables through the tray, then run the desk through its full travel so nothing binds or rattles.

  • Uplift, Fully Jarvis, Autonomous, Vari, FlexiSpot, Branch, IKEA
  • Cable trays, grommets and power strips mounted under the top
  • Memory presets programmed for the user

Desk Assembly

L desks, U desks, executive desks with pedestals, reception desks, writing desks and computer desks with hutches. We build each desk in the spot where it lives so you never have to carry an assembled desk down a hallway.

  • Pedestal drawers aligned so they close flush
  • Hutches and returns squared and secured
  • Keyboard trays and CPU holders mounted

Workstations and Cubicles

Modular workstations, benching systems, panel cubicles and privacy pods that ship in boxes. We lay out the run from your floor plan, connect and level the panels, hang the work surfaces and overhead bins, and square the whole row so it looks like one piece.

  • Two person pods to full rows
  • Privacy screens and desk dividers
  • Reconfigurations when a team grows or moves

Conference and Meeting Tables

Boardroom tables, modular training tables, flip top tables, cafe and break room sets. Large tops are heavy and unforgiving, so we bring two people, assemble the base in place, set the top square and level it before anyone sits down.

  • Power and data grommets cut in and aligned
  • Credenzas and media consoles to match
  • Wall mounted TVs for the room through our TV mounting service

Storage, Shelving, Whiteboards and Monitor Arms

Lateral and vertical filing cabinets, bookcases, lockers, storage cabinets, shelving units and mail sorters, all anchored to the wall where they are tall. We also mount whiteboards, glass boards, corkboards, monitor arms and wall shelves while we are there.

  • Tall units anchored to studs, every time
  • Monitor arms clamped or grommet mounted
  • Whiteboards level and on solid anchors

FAQ

Office Furniture Assembly Questions

The questions office managers ask before they book. If yours is not here, call (475) 500-7126.

How much does office furniture assembly cost in Fairfield County?

We quote one flat rate for the whole job before you book, based on the unit count and what the units are. There is no hourly meter and no trip fee. Send us the order list or the product links and the number of each piece, and you get a single number back for the full install. The price you approve is the price on the invoice, even if a desk fights back.

Can you assemble office furniture after hours or on a weekend so we have no downtime?

Yes. We work 8 AM to 10 PM every day, so evening and weekend installs are normal scheduling, not a special request. A Friday evening or Saturday install means staff come in Monday to finished desks. For multi tenant buildings we coordinate with building management on loading dock and freight elevator times.

Do you provide a certificate of insurance for our building management?

Yes. Alliance Handyman Pros is fully insured and certificates of insurance are available for your building management, landlord or association. Tell us when you book if the building requires one so it is on file before the install date.

What happens to all the boxes and packaging?

We break down every box and bag the foam and plastic as part of the job. Flattened cardboard goes to your building's recycling or loading dock area, and we can confirm disposal logistics in advance for large deliveries. You are not left with a conference room full of cardboard.

Do you assemble motorized standing desks and handle the cable management?

Yes. Standing desks from Uplift, Fully, Autonomous, Vari, FlexiSpot, Branch, IKEA and others are a large share of the office work we do. We assemble the frame and top, mount the control box and keypad, set the frame width, route the power and monitor cables through the cable tray or grommets, and run the desk through its full range to confirm it travels cleanly before we leave.

Can you install cubicles and workstation panels?

Yes, for light commercial panel systems and modular workstations that ship in boxes, including benching desks and small pods. We lay out the run from your floor plan, connect and level the panels, hang the work surfaces and overhead storage, and square the whole run. For large reconfigurations with powered panels we review the plan with you first so the right scope is quoted.

How many office chairs or desks can you assemble in one visit?

As many as you have. A typical office chair takes about 15 to 20 minutes and a standard desk 45 to 60 minutes, so a visit covering ten chairs and five desks is a normal day for us. For larger counts we bring a second crew member so the whole order is finished in one visit instead of being spread across several days.

What office furniture brands do you assemble?

All of them. Herman Miller, Steelcase, Haworth, HON, Knoll, Humanscale, Uplift, Fully, Autonomous, Vari, FlexiSpot, Branch, Bush Business Furniture, Sauder, IKEA (Bekant, Idasen, Trotten, Markus, Galant), Wayfair, Amazon and Costco office lines, plus anything else that arrives flat packed or in boxes.

Do you offer invoicing and net 30 terms for business accounts?

Yes. Business accounts and property managers can be invoiced with net 30 terms, and multiple locations can be consolidated onto one monthly invoice. One off office setups can also be paid by card at booking.

What do we need to have ready before you arrive?

Very little. Have the boxes somewhere we can reach them, a floor plan or a quick sketch of where each desk goes, and the building's access instructions if there is a dock or freight elevator. We stage the boxes by room ourselves, so your staff do not have to drag cartons around before we get there. If the building needs a certificate of insurance or a reserved elevator window, tell us at booking and we take care of it.

Can you fix a standing desk or chair that someone on our team already assembled?

Yes. A desk that wobbles at full height, a chair whose arms or casters never seated, a frame with a pinched control cable, a cabinet that was never anchored: we see all of these and we take them over without a lecture. We square the frame, torque the hardware in sequence, run the reset and leave it working.
