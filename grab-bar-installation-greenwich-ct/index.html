What We Install

Grab Bars for Every Part of the Greenwich Bathroom

The bathroom is the most dangerous room in the house. The CDC reports that about 36 million older adults fall each year, and a large share of those falls happen in the bathroom on wet tile and at the tub wall. A grab bar anchored into solid backing turns the two riskiest moments, stepping into the tub and lowering onto the toilet, into supported movements.

Shower Grab Bars

Horizontal bars along the shower wall for balance and support, positioned at hip height. Anchored into studs behind the tile or into rated toggle anchors where a stud does not line up.

Bathtub Entry Bars

Diagonal or vertical bars at the tub entry, the single most hazardous movement in the bathroom. Turns the one legged step over the tub wall into a supported transfer.

Toilet Side Bars

Bars mounted beside the toilet to support lowering and rising. Removes one of the highest frequency daily balance challenges for older adults. Offset flange mounting available for tile walls.

Fold Down Bars

For smaller Greenwich bathrooms where a fixed bar would block access. Folds flat against the wall when not in use. Common in guest baths shared across generations.

ADA Height Placement

Horizontal bars set between 33 and 36 inches per ADA guidance, confirmed against the person who will use them. Rated for a minimum 250 pound load.

Greenwich Walls

Tile, Fiberglass, and Plaster Over Lath

Greenwich bathrooms run the full range. Newer builds have ceramic tile over cement board or a molded fiberglass surround. Many pre 1960 Greenwich homes still have plaster over lath, which will not hold a grab bar on a standard drywall anchor. We work the wall to match its construction: we find the stud and anchor directly when one lines up, we drill tile with carbide tip bits at low speed so nothing cracks, and where a stud is not in the right place we use toggle anchors rated at 300 lbs per anchor with two anchors per bar end. On plaster we add backing so the bar holds the same load as a stud mounted install.

We never use standard drywall anchors, suction cup bars, or towel bar hardware for a grab bar meant to bear body weight. Every bar is load tested at 250 lbs before we leave, and any bar that shifts under the test gets re anchored on the spot at no charge.
