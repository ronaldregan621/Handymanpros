What We Do

Four Jobs People Ask For

1. Take the TV down

Moving companies often will not touch a wall-mounted TV, and painters work around it. We disconnect the cables, lift the TV off, remove the bracket from the wall and leave you the hardware so it can go back up later. Large TVs are a two-hands-minimum lift and the screens crack easily when carried flat, which is the reason most people call rather than try it alone.

2. Move it to a new wall or a new home

Relocating a TV is really a new install with your existing TV and mount. We find the studs in the new wall, set the height for where you will actually be sitting, mount it level and reconnect everything. If the new wall is different from the old one, for example drywall to brick, the method and the price change, so tell us what the new wall is made of. If the TV is coming from another address, have it and the mount hardware at the new place before we arrive.

3. Put a new TV on the mount you already have

Upgrading from a 55 to a 75? Sometimes the old mount works and sometimes it does not. Three things decide it: the mount's weight rating, whether its bolt pattern (VESA) matches the new TV, and whether the bigger screen will sit at a sensible height or clear the mantle. Text us the model of the new TV and a photo of the current setup. If the mount works, the swap starts at $165. If it does not, we can supply a new mount for $85 and the job is priced as a standard mount from $199.

4. Unbox and set up a new TV

For TVs going on a stand or console rather than the wall. We unbox it, attach the feet, place it, connect your cable box, streaming device, game console and soundbar, power it on and make sure you have a picture. Setup starts at $165.

Wires, soundbars and boxes

A move is a good time to clean up the cords. An exterior cord cover is $50 and in-wall concealment is $175; the details are on our wire concealment page. A soundbar is $85 and a device mounted behind the TV is $90. Any additional hardware or supplies are the client's responsibility.

TV Take-Down and Remounting Questions

Can you take down a TV? +
Yes, we can. We disconnect the cables, remove the TV and the mount from the wall, and leave you the hardware so it can be reinstalled.
Can you move a TV to another wall? +
Yes, we can. Moving a TV is a new install with your existing TV and mount. Text a photo of both walls to (475) 500-7126 and we will confirm your total before you book.
Can you put my new TV on my existing mount? +
Usually, yes. The mount has to be rated for the new TV weight and match its VESA bolt pattern. Text us the new TV model and a photo of the current setup and we will check.
Do you patch the holes where the TV used to be? +
Patching and painting old mount holes is not part of a take-down or remount. If you want the holes patched, ask when you text us and we will tell you before the job.
Can you remount a TV I took down myself? +
Yes, we can. Have the TV, the mount and its hardware at the address. If any bolts or brackets are missing, tell us ahead of time. Any additional hardware or supplies are the client's responsibility.
Do you unbox and set up TVs that are not being wall mounted? +
Yes. Unboxing and setup starts at $165. We unbox the TV, attach the stand, connect your devices and make sure it is working.
How long does a TV take-down or remount take? +
A take-down or a swap onto an existing mount usually takes under an hour. Remounting on a new wall typically takes 1 to 2 hours.
How do I pay? +
We're cashless. Your card is saved when you book, held 24 hours before the appointment, and charged only after the job is done. No deposit.
